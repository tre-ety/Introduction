// About

A short introduction to who I am and what I care about.

I work at the meeting point of strategy, product and politics. As a Strategy and Product Management Trainee at CIRQL, an early-stage startup, I run competitor and market-positioning research using agentic AI tooling, translate user feedback into clearly defined product roadmaps, and drive growth at the coalface.

That product instinct grew out of a political education. I am a Politics graduate of Queen Mary University of London, on track for a First Class degree, with a dissertation on how disinformation, voter predispositions and contested historical memory shaped the 2022 Philippine presidential election.

Alongside this I organise in East London with Citizens UK, serve as Regional Communications, Media and PR Officer for St John Ambulance, and have worked for the better part of a decade as a freelance open-source investigator. I research, build, write, film and organise.
